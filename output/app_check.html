How it was tested: I used the site in the built-in browser like a shopper (clicking, typing in the chat), then checked every number against the database with a direct SQL query. Screenshots are from a phone-width window (about 420 px), which also shows the compact chat panel.

1. Inventory levels of an item

StepsOpened the Yale Dad Hoodie page, opened the chat and asked: "How many of this hoodie do you have in each size?"
What the agent didKnew "this hoodie" from the open page (page context), then called check_stock, which reads the inventory table.
Chat answer"The Yale Dad Hoodie has: XS 5, S 5, M 12, L 20, XL 12, and XXL 15 in stock."
Product pageSizes & stock boxes: XS 5 · S 5 · M 12 · L 20 · XL 12 · XXL 15
DatabaseSELECT size, quantity FROM inventory WHERE product_id = 'yale-dad-hoodie' → XS 5, S 5, M 12, L 20, XL 12, XXL 15
ResultPASS All three agree for all six sizes.

2. Dynamic search result cards

StepsFrom the Home page, asked the chat: "what hoodies do you have?"
What happenedThe agent searched in browse mode (find_products, category hoodie, up to 30) and returned the matching product ids. The backend turned them into cards from the database, and a "From your chat: 27 matching items" section appeared at the top of the page.
Cards27 cards, each with image, name, price, a short description and sizes in stock. 0 broken images. First cards: Basic Hoodie Big Yale $68, Brooks Brothers Double Knit Full Zip Hoodie $88, Champion Full Zip Hood $88.
Database27 products in the hoodie category. Every card clicks through to its product page.
ResultPASS The card count matches the catalogue, with no made-up products.

3. Usability feature: suggestion chips and live status

Suggestion chipsAn empty chat shows 🎁 Gift ideas · 🍂 Fall layers · 🏈 Football season. The seasonal and sport chips change with the month (e.g. ❄️ / 🏒 in winter, ⚾ in spring and summer, 🏟️ The Game in November). Tapping one sends its question.
Live statusWhile the agent works, the chat shows its real current step, streamed from the backend (not a timer): 💭 Thinking → 🔎 Searching the catalogue → 📦 Checking stock → ✍️ Writing your answer, with "Hang tight, your results are almost here! 🐾".
Compact phone chatOn small screens the chat is a 280×340 box in the lower right (about 30% of the screen), so the page and its cards stay visible.
ResultPASS
