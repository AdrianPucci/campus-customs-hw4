## 1. Matching items appear on the page

- **What:** asking "what hoodies do you have?" fills a **"From your chat: N matching items"** section at the top of the page with full cards (image, name, price, short description, sizes in stock). Up to 4 also show inside the chat.
- **Why:** shoppers can see and compare items instead of reading a long list in a small chat bubble.
- **Detail:** every card opens the product page. On a product page the list shrinks to a "← Back to your N matching items" bar so the large image stays in view, and every page opens at the top.

## 2. Suggestion chips that change with the season

- **What:** an empty chat offers tappable starters: **🎁 Gift ideas**, a seasonal pick (🍂 Fall layers, ❄️ Cozy winter, 🌸 Spring layers, ☀️ Summer tees, 🏟️ The Game in November) and the **Yale sport in season** (🏈 football in fall, 🏒 hockey in winter, ⚾ baseball in spring and summer).
- **Why:** a blank chat box makes people guess what to ask; one tap gets a useful answer.

🛑 **Adrian:**

## 3. Live "what I'm doing" status

- **What:** while the agent works, the chat shows its **real current step**, streamed from the backend: 💭 Thinking → 🔎 Searching the catalogue → 📦 Checking stock by size → ✍️ Writing your answer, with "Hang tight, your results are almost here! 🐾".
- **Why:** answers take 3-8 seconds; seeing progress makes the wait feel shorter and shows the answer comes from real lookups.

🛑 **Adrian:**

## 4. Compact chat on phones

- **What:** on small screens the chat is a small box in the lower right instead of covering the page.
- **Why:** the cards the agent finds appear on the page behind the chat, so they have to stay visible.

🛑 **Adrian:**

## 5. Understands the page and casual wording

- **"This" / "it"** means the product page that's open ("does this come in gray?").
- Casual spelling works ("sweats shirts", "hoodys", "tees"); "the store" means the online store.
- Color, price and sort requests work ("cheapest hoodie", "cream crewnecks", "under $40"); for colors we don't carry (pink), it offers the closest real ones.

🛑 **Adrian:**

## 6. Accounts and saved chats

- Create account with **confirm password** and clear error messages; log in with email and password; "Hi, <name>" and Log Out in the nav.
- Logged-in shoppers' chats are **saved and reloaded** ("Welcome back, …"); guests' chats are not saved.

🛑 **Adrian:**

## 7. Friendly safety messages

- Too many messages: "Whoa, Bulldog! 🐾 … try again in N seconds."
- Card number, SSN or password typed in chat: removed right away, with a 🔒 notice.
- A line under the chat: "AI assistant · can make mistakes · never share payment info".
